# Pustaka Lighting

Cahaya adalah pengungkit terkuat dalam prompt foto. Mengubah satu frasa cahaya
mengubah suasana lebih drastis daripada mengubah background, pose, atau warna.
Kalau hasil generasi terasa datar dan "murah", penyebabnya hampir selalu cahaya —
bukan resolusi.

## Cara kerja cahaya dalam prompt

Deskripsi cahaya yang berguna menjawab tiga hal. Menyebut ketiganya membuat hasil
konsisten dan bisa diulang; menyebut satu saja membuat model mengisi sisanya
sesuka hati.

1. **Dari mana** — arah dan ketinggian sumbernya
2. **Sekeras apa** — tepi bayangan tajam atau lembut
3. **Seterang apa bayangannya** — beda terang antara sisi terang dan sisi gelap

> *Soft light from a large source at camera left, 45 degrees above eye level, with
> gentle fill on the shadow side so the contrast stays moderate.*

Perhatikan: tidak satu pun kata di kalimat itu adalah jargon. Model gambar
memahami deskripsi fisik cahaya jauh lebih andal daripada nama merek peralatan.
Nama pola seperti "Rembrandt" berguna sebagai singkatan, tapi paling aman dipakai
**bersama** penjabaran fisiknya.

## Pola cahaya potret

Nama-nama klasik dari studio fotografi. Semuanya soal ke mana bayangan hidung jatuh.

| Pola | Kalimat prompt | Kesan |
|---|---|---|
| **Rembrandt** | Rembrandt lighting — key light 45 degrees to the side and above, creating a small illuminated triangle on the shadow-side cheek | Klasik, berkarakter, sedikit dramatis |
| **Loop** | Loop lighting — key light slightly off-axis, casting a small downward loop of shadow from the nose onto the cheek | Paling serbaguna untuk potret |
| **Split** | Split lighting — key light directly to one side at 90 degrees, dividing the face into a fully lit half and a fully shadowed half | Tegas, misterius, maskulin |
| **Butterfly / Paramount** | Butterfly lighting — key light directly in front and high above, casting a small symmetrical shadow beneath the nose | Glamor, klasik Hollywood |
| **Clamshell** | Clamshell lighting — a soft light above the face and a reflector or second soft light below, wrapping the face evenly | Beauty, kulit rata, minim bayangan |
| **Broad** | Broad lighting — the side of the face turned toward the camera is the lit side | Memperlebar wajah |
| **Short** | Short lighting — the side of the face turned away from the camera is the lit side | Menirus kan dan membentuk wajah |
| **Rim / edge** | Rim lighting — a bright edge of light tracing the outline of the subject, separating them from a dark background | Memisahkan subjek, sinematik |
| **Silhouette** | Backlit silhouette — the subject rendered as a dark shape against a bright background, with no detail on their front | Grafis, penuh suasana |

## Kualitas cahaya

Ini soal ukuran sumber cahaya relatif terhadap subjek. Sumber besar dan dekat =
lembut. Kecil dan jauh = keras. Selalu sebut salah satunya.

| Kualitas | Kalimat prompt |
|---|---|
| Sangat lembut | Very soft light from a large diffused source, with shadows that fade almost imperceptibly |
| Lembut | Soft light with gently graded shadow edges |
| Sedang | Moderately soft light with defined but not harsh shadow edges |
| Keras | Hard light from a small source, with crisp, sharply defined shadow edges |
| Sangat keras | Harsh direct light casting stark, high-contrast shadows with knife-sharp edges |
| Menyebar | Fully diffused ambient light with no single identifiable direction |
| Spekular | Specular light producing bright, tight highlights on the skin and in the eyes |

## Rasio & mood

Seberapa gelap sisi bayangan. Ini yang menentukan foto terasa ceria atau berat.

| Mood | Kalimat prompt |
|---|---|
| High key | High-key lighting — bright, airy, low contrast, with pale shadows and a light background |
| Low key | Low-key lighting — predominantly dark, with a small pool of light on the subject and deep surrounding shadow |
| Chiaroscuro | Chiaroscuro lighting with a dramatic interplay of deep shadow and a single strong light, in the manner of a Baroque painting |
| Kontras tinggi | High-contrast lighting with bright highlights and dense shadows, minimal fill |
| Kontras rendah | Low-contrast lighting with an even, gentle tonal range throughout |
| Datar | Flat even lighting with no visible shadow, as used for documents and catalogs |
| Moody | Moody lighting with restrained exposure, rich shadow detail, and a single motivated light source |

## Resep cepat per kebutuhan

| Kebutuhan | Rangkaian cahaya |
|---|---|
| Headshot LinkedIn | Large softbox key at 45 degrees, gentle fill opposite, subtle rim light, moderate contrast |
| Beauty / kecantikan | Clamshell lighting with an octabox above and a reflector below, soft and even |
| Potret dramatis pria | Short lighting with hard key, negative fill on the shadow side, and a cool rim light |
| Potret hangat keluarga | Soft overcast daylight or large window light, low contrast, warm neutral balance |
| Foto produk | Two large diffused sources at 45 degrees each side, plus a soft gradient on the background |
| Makanan | Soft directional side or back light to reveal texture, with a white bounce card filling the front |
| Interior properti | Balanced ambient interior light with the window exposure held, no harsh single source |
| Foto dokumen | Flat even frontal lighting with no visible shadow |
| Sinematik malam | Cool ambient base with warm motivated practicals and a strong rim separating the subject |

## Memperbaiki cahaya yang meleset

| Gejala | Tambahkan ke prompt |
|---|---|
| Wajah datar tanpa dimensi | `Add clear directional key light from one side so the face has a lit side and a shadow side` |
| Bayangan terlalu gelap | `Add soft fill light on the shadow side to retain detail` |
| Terlihat seperti lampu kilat HP | `Replace the flat on-camera flash look with soft directional light from a large source to the side` |
| Kulit mengkilap berlebihan | `Reduce specular highlights on the skin; use diffused light and keep the skin matte` |
| Kontras berlebihan | `Lower the lighting ratio — keep the highlights and shadows closer in brightness` |
| Warna kulit aneh | `Neutralize the mixed color temperature and restore accurate skin tones` |
| Latar gelap menelan subjek | `Add a rim light behind the subject to separate them from the background` |
| Mata mati, tanpa kilau | `Add a visible catchlight in both eyes from the key light` |
| Terlihat seperti HDR palsu | `Remove the HDR look — allow natural highlight rolloff and genuinely dark shadows` |
