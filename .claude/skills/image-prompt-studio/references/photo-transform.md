# Korpus Transformasi Foto

Prompt siap pakai untuk mengubah foto yang sudah ada. Semua ditulis untuk mode
**EDIT**, jadi semuanya sudah mengandung identity lock. Ambil yang paling dekat
dengan kebutuhan user, lalu sesuaikan — jangan tempel mentah-mentah kalau ada
detail spesifik di foto yang layak disebut.

## Enam prompt inti

Ini set kanonik — pendek, seimbang, dan terbukti. Kekuatannya justru pada
kependekannya: satu kalimat aksi, satu kalimat lock. Jadikan ini titik awal
sebelum menambah apa pun.

### 1. Upgrade to DSLR Visual
Foto HP biasa jadi terasa diambil kamera profesional. Preset paling serbaguna —
kalau user cuma bilang "bikin bagusan dong", mulai dari sini.

> Transform my photo to look like it was taken with a professional full-frame
> DSLR camera. Keep my face, body, expression, clothing, and identity exactly the
> same. Add realistic depth of field, natural skin texture, a slight grain, and
> balanced exposure. Do not beautify or alter any shape.

### 2. Luxury Editorial Style
Untuk foto yang mau terasa seperti halaman majalah — kontras tegas, subjek
terpisah bersih dari background.

> Transform my photo into a high-end editorial portrait. Keep my facial structure,
> body, and skin texture unchanged. Enhance the lighting, composition, contrast,
> and background separation to make it look like a luxury magazine photoshoot.

### 3. External Edit in Golden Hour
Menempelkan cahaya matahari sore yang hangat ke foto luar ruangan. Paling berhasil
kalau foto aslinya memang di luar dan cahayanya datar.

> Transform my photo into a natural golden hour photo. Keep my exact identity.
> Add warm and realistic sunlight, soft shadows, natural skin tones, and a slight
> depth of field. Avoid artificial shine or exaggerated saturation.

### 4. Clean Professional Photo (Headshot)
Foto profil untuk LinkedIn, CV, profil kantor, atau situs perusahaan.

> Create a clean professional photo (headshot) from my photo. Keep my face, hair,
> and expression identical. Use natural and soft lighting, realistic texture, and
> a slight blur in the background. No beautification.

### 5. Correction of Poor Lighting
Menyelamatkan foto yang backlight, gelap sebelah, atau overexposed. Ini preset
perbaikan, bukan gaya — jangan gabung dengan grading berat.

> Fix the lighting in my photo without redoing my face. Keep my identity,
> features, and skin texture exactly the same. Balance the harsh shadows, recover
> the dark or overexposed parts, and make the lighting look natural, as if the
> photo had been taken in a well-lit environment. No smoothing or beautifying.

### 6. Professional Neutral Background
Mengganti latar berantakan tanpa membuat subjek terlihat seperti tempelan.
Kalimat soal tepi rambut penting — di situ ganti background biasanya ketahuan.

> Replace the messy background in my photo with a professional and neutral
> background (a soft solid tone or a subtle studio blur). Keep my face, hair,
> clothing, and the lighting on me exactly the same, and make the transition
> between me and the new background look realistic, without artificial edges
> around the hair.

## Turunan: profesional & karier

### 7. Corporate Headshot — Formal
> Turn this into a formal corporate headshot. Keep my face, expression, hair, and
> glasses exactly as they are. Place me against a clean dark grey gradient
> backdrop, lit with soft key light from camera left and a subtle rim light
> separating my shoulder from the background. Chest-up framing at eye level.
> Preserve skin pores and natural texture. No beautification, no slimming.

### 8. Startup / Tech Profile — Relaxed
> Restyle this as a relaxed professional portrait for a tech company's team page.
> Keep my identity, face, and clothing unchanged. Place me in a bright modern
> office with a softly blurred background, natural window light from the side,
> and a warm neutral color balance. Keep my expression exactly as it is. No
> retouching of skin or facial features.

### 9. Speaker / Conference Portrait
> Make this look like a professional conference speaker portrait. Keep my facial
> structure, expression, and clothing identical. Add controlled directional
> lighting with a soft shadow on one side, a dark uncluttered background, and
> shallow depth of field. Slight contrast increase only. Do not smooth skin or
> reshape the face.

### 10. Academic / Formal Document Photo
> Convert this into a formal ID-style portrait. Keep my face, hair, and expression
> completely unchanged. Use flat, even frontal lighting with no visible shadows,
> a plain light blue background, straight-on eye-level framing, and sharp focus
> across the whole face. Do not retouch, smooth, or alter any feature — this must
> remain an accurate likeness.

### 11. Team Photo Consistency
Berguna saat beberapa foto anggota tim diambil di kondisi berbeda-beda.
> Match this portrait to a consistent studio look: neutral grey backdrop, soft
> frontal key light with gentle fill, neutral white balance, and chest-up framing
> at eye level. Keep each person's face, expression, clothing, and skin tone
> exactly as photographed. Only lighting, background, and framing may change.

## Turunan: sosial & personal

### 12. Cinematic Portrait
> Restyle this photo with a cinematic film look. Keep my identity, facial
> structure, and skin texture exactly the same. Add soft directional key light,
> deep but detailed shadows, a subtle teal-and-orange color grade, gentle 35mm
> film grain, and a widescreen sense of depth. No exaggerated saturation, no
> crushed blacks, no beautification.

### 13. Black & White Fine Art
> Convert this to a fine art black and white portrait. Keep my face, expression,
> and skin texture exactly as they are. Use rich tonal separation with true blacks
> and clean whites, soft directional light shaping the face, and visible film
> grain. Preserve every skin detail, mole, and line — the character of the face is
> the subject.

### 14. Blue Hour / Night City
> Place me in a blue hour city setting. Keep my face, body, clothing, and
> expression completely unchanged. Add cool ambient twilight light on me with warm
> practical lights from shop signs behind, shallow depth of field turning the
> background lights into soft bokeh, and a natural mix of warm and cool tones. No
> artificial glow on my skin.

### 15. Soft Natural Window Light
> Relight this photo as if taken beside a large window on an overcast day. Keep my
> identity, features, and skin texture unchanged. Soft wraparound light from one
> side, a gentle falloff into shadow, neutral colors, and no harsh highlights.
> Keep the original background but let it fall slightly out of focus.

### 16. Travel Documentary
> Restyle this as a documentary travel photograph. Keep my face, clothing, and
> pose exactly as they are. Preserve the real environment behind me but improve
> its clarity and depth, add natural available light, slightly desaturated
> realistic colors, and fine grain. It should look observed, not posed or
> retouched.

### 17. Family Portrait Warmth
> Warm this family photo into a natural, timeless portrait. Keep every person's
> face, expression, and clothing exactly as they are — this is the whole point of
> the photo. Even out the lighting across all faces, add gentle warmth, soften
> harsh shadows, and improve overall clarity. Do not beautify or alter anyone.

## Turunan: perbaikan teknis

### 18. Old Photo Restoration
Satu-satunya preset di mana model memang boleh merekonstruksi.
> Restore this damaged old photograph. Repair the scratches, tears, creases, dust,
> and faded areas, and correct the color shift back to neutral. Reconstruct only
> the physically damaged regions — leave every intact area, especially the faces,
> exactly as they are. Keep the original grain and the character of the era. Do
> not modernize, sharpen aggressively, or beautify anyone's features.

### 19. Colorize Black & White
> Add realistic, natural color to this black and white photograph. Keep every
> facial feature, expression, and detail exactly as it is — only color is added.
> Use believable period-appropriate skin tones, clothing colors, and ambient
> light. Keep the colors slightly restrained rather than vivid, and preserve the
> original grain.

### 20. Denoise & Recover Detail
> Clean up the noise in this low-light photo while keeping it looking like a real
> photograph. Keep my face and identity unchanged. Reduce chroma noise, recover
> shadow detail, and restore natural color, but retain fine luminance grain and
> genuine skin texture. No smoothing, no waxy skin, no oversharpening halos.

### 21. Fix Color Cast
> Correct the color cast in this photo. Keep everything else identical. Neutralize
> the unnatural tint from the artificial lighting, restore accurate skin tones and
> true whites, and keep the overall brightness as it is. Do not change contrast,
> composition, or any facial feature.

### 22. Unblur / Sharpen Realistically
> Improve the sharpness and clarity of this slightly soft photo. Keep my face,
> features, and expression exactly the same. Recover fine detail in the eyes,
> hair, and fabric naturally, as though the original had been in focus. Avoid
> halos, artificial edge contrast, and any change to facial structure.

### 23. Recompose & Extend
> Extend this photo outward to a 16:9 frame. Keep me and everything already in the
> frame exactly as they are, without rescaling or repositioning me. Continue the
> existing background naturally into the new areas, matching its perspective,
> lighting, focus falloff, and grain.

## Turunan: produk & bisnis

### 24. E-commerce Product on White
> Turn this into a clean e-commerce product photo. Keep the product's exact shape,
> color, material, texture, branding, and every label detail unchanged. Place it
> on a pure white seamless background with soft even studio lighting, a subtle
> natural contact shadow beneath it, and sharp focus throughout. No reflections
> that distort the product, no color shift.

### 25. Lifestyle Product Scene
> Place this product in a warm lifestyle setting. Keep the product itself
> completely unchanged in shape, color, texture, and labeling. Surround it with a
> realistic tabletop scene, soft natural window light from the side, shallow depth
> of field, and complementary muted props that do not compete with it.

### 26. Food Photography
> Restyle this dish as professional food photography. Keep the actual food, its
> portion, arrangement, and colors truthful to the original — do not add, remove,
> or idealize any ingredient. Add soft directional side light that shows texture
> and steam, a gently blurred background, and natural appetizing color. No
> artificial gloss.

### 27. Real Estate Interior
> Improve this interior photo to real estate listing quality. Keep the room's
> actual layout, furniture, and proportions exactly as they are. Straighten the
> vertical lines, balance the window exposure against the interior, brighten the
> shadows naturally, and neutralize the mixed lighting color. Do not add,
> remove, or restyle anything in the room.

### 28. UMKM Catalog Shot
> Turn this phone photo of my product into a simple, credible catalog image. Keep
> the product exactly as it is, including any imperfections. Replace the cluttered
> surroundings with a plain soft-toned backdrop, add even diffused lighting and a
> soft natural shadow, and straighten the framing. Keep it honest — the buyer
> should receive what they see.

## Cara memodifikasi preset

Empat pengungkit, urut dari yang paling sering dibutuhkan:

**1. Ganti sumber cahaya.** Tukar frasa cahaya dengan entri dari
`references/lighting.md`. Ini mengubah suasana lebih kuat daripada pengungkit lain.

**2. Ganti jarak dan sudut.** Tukar frasa framing dengan kode dari
`references/camera-codes.md`. Perhatikan: pada mode EDIT, mengubah sudut secara
drastis memaksa model menggambar ulang wajah dari sudut yang tidak ada di foto
asli — risiko kehilangan kemiripan tinggi. Untuk edit, tetap pada sudut asli atau
bergeser sedikit saja; kode sudut bebas dipakai penuh pada mode GENERATE.

**3. Tambah "rasa".** Satu, maksimal dua entri dari
`references/visual-effects.md`. Lebih dari dua saling bertabrakan dan hasilnya
jadi berlumur efek.

**4. Perkuat lock.** Kalau hasilnya bagus tapi orangnya berubah, jangan ubah
gayanya — naikkan tingkat lock di `references/identity-lock.md`.

**Menyusun ulang preset jadi permintaan baru:** ambil kalimat aksi dari satu
preset, kalimat lock dari preset lain, dan tambahkan satu detail spesifik dari
foto yang dilihat. Detail spesifik itu yang biasanya membuat hasilnya terasa
dibuat khusus, bukan hasil template.
